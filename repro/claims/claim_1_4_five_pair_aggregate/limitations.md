# Limitations and deviations

- This is endpoint evidence only; it does not test the paper's nine-direction
  eigenvalue-rank trend.
- The endpoint runs use the accepted one-million-network geometry estimate.
- All five pairs favor the reported direction, but five seeds still give
  limited uncertainty resolution. The exact bootstrap is reported without
  treating it as proof of a universal ordering.
- The smallest paper-scale individual eigenvector is unstable across
  independent half-estimates. No causal rank interpretation is assigned to
  that bottom direction, although the full-estimator vector is used unchanged.
