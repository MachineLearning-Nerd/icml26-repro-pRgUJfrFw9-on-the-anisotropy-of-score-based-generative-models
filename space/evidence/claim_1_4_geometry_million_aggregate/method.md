# Method — paper-scale geometry integration

The two frozen half-million matrices are decoded only after their payload
hashes and provenance contracts pass. Because the shards have equal sample
counts, their arithmetic mean is exactly the mean of all one million score
outer products.

The verifier reconstructs the descending eigendecomposition, audits residuals
and orthonormality, and compares each selected individual eigenvector and local
nine-vector band across the two independent 500,000-network shard matrices.
The individual-vector and band thresholds were inherited from the earlier
direction gate before cross-shard results were inspected.

Four independent parity-half matrices, each containing 250,000 networks, drive
a fixed-seed bootstrap for selected eigenvalue intervals. An independent
checker separately decodes both source matrices and reconstructs the aggregate
and spectrum without importing the primary verifier.
